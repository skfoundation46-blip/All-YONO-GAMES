Playing points rummy

Points rummy follows the standard 13-card Indian rummy format used by many apps. You join a table, receive cards and try to form valid pure sequences and sets. The player with the lowest valid points when someone declares usually wins the round.

Practical points for any points rummy table:

  • Choose a table with an entry fee or point value you are comfortable losing.
  • Form a pure sequence first. Without it you normally cannot declare.
  • Watch the discard pile. Other players’ discards can help or hurt your hand.
  • Declare only when you are sure the hand is valid. Wrong declarations commonly carry a penalty.
  • Point values and maximum loss per round vary by table. The app shows the exact rules for the table you join.
