How Points, Pool and Deals Rummy work on Rummy77

Pool Rummy (usually 101 or 201) continues until a player’s score reaches the pool limit; the last player standing wins the pot. Deals Rummy is played over a fixed number of deals; the player with the highest chip count at the end takes the prize.
