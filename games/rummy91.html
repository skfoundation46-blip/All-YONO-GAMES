How Points Rummy and Pool Rummy work

Points Rummy: Each table has a fixed rupee value per point. You receive 13 cards. Arrange them into valid sequences and sets (at least one pure sequence without joker is required). The first player to declare validly scores zero points. Other players pay based on the points of their remaining cards. A round usually finishes in a few minutes.

Pool Rummy (101 / 201): Players accumulate points across multiple rounds. When a player crosses the pool limit (101 or 201), they are eliminated. The last remaining player wins the pool. Drop rules and scoring follow standard Indian rummy conventions. Both formats require a pure sequence for a legal declaration.

Other modes (Teen Patti, Andar Bahar, etc.) may appear in the same app but carry different risk profiles. Always read the table rules before joining a cash game.
