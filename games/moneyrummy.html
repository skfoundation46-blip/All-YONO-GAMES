How Points Rummy and Pool Rummy Work

Standard Indian rummy rules apply in most real-money versions. Each player receives 13 cards. The goal is to form valid melds: at least two sequences (one of them a pure sequence without jokers) and any remaining sets. A pure sequence is mandatory for a valid declaration.

Points Rummy (single-deal format)

  1. Choose a table with a fixed rupee value per point (common on these platforms).
  2. Play one deal. Draw from the closed or open pile, then discard.
  3. The first player to declare a valid hand wins. Unmatched cards of the losers are scored (face cards usually 10 points, number cards face value, jokers 0).
  4. The winner receives the sum of opponents’ points multiplied by the point value, minus any platform fee.

Points rummy is the fastest format—often finished in a few minutes.

Pool Rummy (multi-round elimination)

  1. Players buy into a fixed entry that forms the prize pool (common variants are 101 and 201 pool).
  2. Scores accumulate across rounds. A player is eliminated when they reach the pool limit.
  3. The last remaining player (or the one with the lowest score when the game ends) wins the pool.
  4. Dropping early usually costs a fixed penalty (for example 20 points in 101 pool).

Confirm the exact drop scores, fees, and available variants inside the current app lobby, as operators can adjust them.
