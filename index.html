How to use the list

The cards show a bonus label and a minimum withdrawal note. These are directory labels only. They can change and are not guarantees. Confirm the live offer and rules inside the app or on the publisher’s own pages.

What to check before installing

Prefer a recognised source. Check the exact app name, publisher identity and domain spelling. For APK files, keep Android security features enabled and do not ignore warnings. Review the permissions the app requests.
